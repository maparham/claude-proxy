Questions

What does a share mean?

Your ticket reserves that percentage of the subscription. Your limits are that share of Anthropic’s 5-hour window, and one seventh of it per ticket day.

What happens when I reach my limit?

The request that crosses the limit is still served. After that, requests are refused until the 5-hour window or the ticket day resets.

Sonnet or Opus?

Both are included. Opus uses your share faster, so the cards show fewer hours for it.

What happens when my ticket ends?

Tickets don’t renew automatically. Access stops when the ticket ends, and you buy a new one to continue.
